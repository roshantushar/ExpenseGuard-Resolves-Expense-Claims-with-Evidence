## IN-1.1 - Scope and precedence

This addendum applies to transactions incurred in India. Where it differs from a global policy for the same subject, this addendum controls, and a Finance circular that names one of its clauses amends that clause from the effective date.

Interpretive guidance: This addendum serves to clarify the applicability of certain transactions incurred in India, establishing its precedence over global policies when discrepancies arise. Its purpose is to ensure that employees operating in India adhere to specific guidelines that may differ from the overarching corporate policies, thereby accommodating local regulations and business practices. Typical situations addressed by this clause include expense reporting, compliance with local financial regulations, and any amendments necessitated by the unique operational landscape in India, ensuring that employees remain compliant while conducting business activities.

A common misunderstanding regarding this clause is the belief that it allows for arbitrary deviations from global policies without proper justification. Employees may misinterpret the precedence established by this addendum, leading to potential non-compliance with corporate standards. This clause is conceptually linked to other regional policies and amendments, emphasizing the importance of understanding local contexts while still adhering to the fundamental principles of the company's overall governance framework.

## IN-2.1 - Employee meal ceiling

| Year | Ceiling (INR) |
|---|---|
| 2024 | 1,800 |
| 2025 | 2,000 |
| 2026 | 2,200 |

Interpretive guidance: This clause establishes a ceiling for employee-only meal expenses incurred in India, providing a clear guideline for permissible spending in this category. Employees may mistakenly believe that they can exceed the established ceiling if they deem the expense necessary, which could lead to potential reimbursement issues. This clause is conceptually related to other expense policies, such as those governing travel and entertainment, highlighting the importance of maintaining clear boundaries for various types of expenditures to ensure compliance and accountability in financial reporting.

It is particularly applicable in situations where employees are required to travel for meetings, conferences, or other work-related engagements. This topic also intersects with the approval thresholds for expenses, as both require managerial oversight to ensure that expenditures are justified and within the company’s financial parameters.

Understanding these distinctions is crucial for employees to ensure compliance with the company's policies and to avoid delays in the reimbursement process.
