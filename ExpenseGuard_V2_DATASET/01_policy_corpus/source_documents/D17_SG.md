## SG-1.1 - Scope and precedence

This addendum applies to transactions incurred in Singapore. Where it differs from a global policy for the same subject, this addendum controls, and a Finance circular that names one of its clauses amends that clause from the effective date.

Interpretive guidance: This clause establishes the applicability and precedence of the addendum concerning transactions incurred in Singapore. It is essential for ensuring that employees are aware that in instances where the addendum diverges from global policies, the local guidelines take precedence. This is particularly important in business situations where employees may engage in activities that are subject to both local and global policies, as it provides clarity on which set of rules governs their conduct and expense reporting.

A potential area of confusion may arise when employees encounter discrepancies between the addendum and global policies. Some may assume that global policies are universally applicable, not recognizing the specific authority granted to local guidelines in this context. This clause is conceptually linked to the overarching governance of corporate policies, as it emphasizes the need for localized adaptations while maintaining alignment with global standards, thereby ensuring compliance with local regulations and practices.

## SG-2.1 - Employee meal ceiling

| Year | Ceiling (SGD) |
|---|---|

This is particularly relevant in situations where employees are entertaining clients or attending business meetings that involve meals, as it provides a clear guideline to ensure that expenses remain reasonable and justifiable.

A common misunderstanding regarding this clause may involve the assumption that all meal expenses can be claimed without regard for the specified limits. This clause is conceptually related to other policies regarding expense limits and entertainment, as it highlights the importance of fiscal responsibility and adherence to established guidelines in managing corporate resources effectively.

## SG-2.4 - Alcohol

Alcohol is permitted only with external attendees and may not exceed 35 percent of the total bill in 2024 and 2025, and 30 percent in 2026.

Interpretive guidance: The purpose of this clause is to regulate the consumption of alcohol during business-related events, ensuring that it is only provided in the presence of external attendees. This policy is particularly relevant during client dinners, corporate celebrations, or networking events, where the presence of alcohol may enhance social interactions. By limiting the proportion of the total bill that can be attributed to alcohol, the company aims to promote responsible consumption and maintain a professional image.

Common misunderstandings may arise from the perception that the percentage limit applies to all expenses within the event. However, it is essential to recognize that this limit specifically pertains to the alcohol portion of the total bill, not the overall expenditure. This clause is conceptually related to other hospitality policies, such as those governing entertainment and meal expenses, which collectively aim to ensure that corporate hospitality remains appropriate and aligns with the company's values.
