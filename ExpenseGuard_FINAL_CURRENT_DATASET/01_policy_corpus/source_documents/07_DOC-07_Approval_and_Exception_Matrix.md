# Approval and Exception Matrix

Document ID: DOC-07
Region: GLOBAL
Effective: 2024-01-01 to 2099-12-31
Category: approval

> Internal synthetic policy for PE6201 ExpenseGuard. Historical documents may remain authoritative for transactions during their effective dates.

## APR-1.1 - Standard manager approval

Any single discretionary expense above SGD 500 equivalent requires manager approval unless a stricter category or regional threshold applies.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## APR-1.2 - Director approval

Any single discretionary expense above SGD 1500 equivalent requires director approval.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## APR-1.3 - Finance review

Exceptions to a prohibited category, conflicts between approvals and transaction facts, or claims above SGD 5000 equivalent require Finance review.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## APR-2.1 - Approval scope

An approval is valid only if it names the employee or expense, the relevant category, and covers the transaction date.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## APR-2.2 - Exception scope

An exception cannot be reused for another employee, category, project, or date range unless explicitly stated.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## APR-3.1 - Missing approval

If a policy requires an approval and no authoritative approval record exists, request information when the employee could supply it; escalate when policy requires Finance-only approval.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.
