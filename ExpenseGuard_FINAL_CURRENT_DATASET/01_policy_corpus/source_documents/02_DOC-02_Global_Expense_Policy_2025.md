# Global Expense Policy 2025

Document ID: DOC-02
Region: GLOBAL
Effective: 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31
Category: general

> Internal synthetic policy for PE6201 ExpenseGuard. Historical documents may remain authoritative for transactions during their effective dates.

## GEP25-1.1 - Business purpose

Expenses must primarily support company business. Mixed personal and business expenses require itemisation; only the documented business portion may be considered.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP25-1.2 - Documentation

Merchant, date, currency, total, and business purpose are mandatory. Category-specific policies may require additional fields.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP25-2.1 - Submission window

Claims must be submitted within 45 calendar days. Claims 46-90 days old require manager approval; claims older than 90 days require Finance escalation.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP25-2.2 - Currency conversion

Use the corporate-card posted base-currency amount when present. Manual FX conversion must cite the Finance monthly rate table.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP25-3.1 - Policy precedence

Regional addenda override global category rules where they explicitly differ. A dated Finance circular overrides the earlier policy for transactions on or after its effective date.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP25-4.1 - Insufficient evidence

If a mandatory fact cannot be determined from the claim or approved enterprise records, request the missing information; do not infer it.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP25-5.1 - Historical use

This policy applies only to 2025 transactions unless a later circular states retrospective effect.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.
