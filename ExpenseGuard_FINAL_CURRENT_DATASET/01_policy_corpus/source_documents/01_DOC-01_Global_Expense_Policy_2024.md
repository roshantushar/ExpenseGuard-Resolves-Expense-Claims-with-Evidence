# Global Expense Policy 2024

Document ID: DOC-01
Region: GLOBAL
Effective: 2024-01-01 to 2024-12-31
Category: general

> Internal synthetic policy for PE6201 ExpenseGuard. Historical documents may remain authoritative for transactions during their effective dates.

## GEP24-1.1 - Business purpose

Expenses must have a clear business purpose directly connected to company activity. Personal benefit alone is not reimbursable.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP24-1.2 - Documentation

Claims must include merchant, transaction date, currency, total amount, and sufficient description to determine the business purpose.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP24-2.1 - Submission window

Claims must be submitted within 60 calendar days of the transaction date unless an approved exception exists.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP24-2.2 - Currency conversion

Foreign-currency claims use the corporate-card posted SGD equivalent when available; otherwise Finance uses the monthly treasury rate.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP24-3.1 - Policy precedence

Legal restrictions and regional addenda override this global policy. Approved written exceptions override category limits only when the exception explicitly names the category and date range.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP24-4.1 - Human review

Where two policies conflict and precedence cannot be determined from published text, the claim must be escalated to Finance rather than guessed.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP24-5.1 - Historical use

This 2024 policy remains authoritative for transactions dated in 2024 even after later versions are published.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.
