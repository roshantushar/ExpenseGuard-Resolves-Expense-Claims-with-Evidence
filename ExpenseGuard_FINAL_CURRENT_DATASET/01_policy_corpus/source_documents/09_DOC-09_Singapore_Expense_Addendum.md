# Singapore Expense Addendum

Document ID: DOC-09
Region: SG
Effective: 2024-01-01 to 2099-12-31
Category: regional

> Internal synthetic policy for PE6201 ExpenseGuard. Historical documents may remain authoritative for transactions during their effective dates.

## SG-1.1 - Employee meal ceiling

Singapore employee-only meal ceiling is SGD 45 per person in 2024, SGD 50 in 2025, and SGD 55 in 2026.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## SG-1.2 - Client meal ceiling

Singapore client meal ceiling is SGD 120 per attendee in 2024-2025 and SGD 130 in 2026.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## SG-1.3 - Entertainment ceiling

Singapore external business entertainment ceiling is SGD 180 per external attendee; manager approval is required above SGD 500 total.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## SG-2.1 - Alcohol

Alcohol is permitted only with external attendees and must not exceed 35 percent of the total meal bill.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## SG-3.1 - Taxi

Taxi and ride-hailing within Singapore are reimbursable for business travel. Home-to-office commute remains non-reimbursable except under the late-night rule.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## SG-4.1 - Regional precedence

Where this addendum differs from the global meals or transport policy, this addendum controls for Singapore transactions.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.
