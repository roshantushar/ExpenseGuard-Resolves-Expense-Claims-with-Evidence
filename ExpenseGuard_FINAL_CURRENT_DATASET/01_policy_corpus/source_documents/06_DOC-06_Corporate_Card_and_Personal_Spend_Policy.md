# Corporate Card and Personal Spend Policy

Document ID: DOC-06
Region: GLOBAL
Effective: 2024-01-01 to 2099-12-31
Category: card

> Internal synthetic policy for PE6201 ExpenseGuard. Historical documents may remain authoritative for transactions during their effective dates.

## CARD-1.1 - Personal spend

Purely personal purchases are not reimbursable even when paid using a corporate card.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## CARD-1.2 - Accidental personal card use

An accidental personal purchase on a corporate card must be rejected for reimbursement and routed to the repayment process outside this project.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## CARD-2.1 - Subscriptions

Software subscriptions require a business owner and, above the approval threshold, a valid project or cost-centre approval.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## CARD-2.2 - Consumer services

Streaming, gaming, personal fitness, household utilities, and other consumer services are presumed personal unless a category policy explicitly permits the specific business use.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## CARD-3.1 - Split transactions

Multiple same-merchant expenses close in time must not be split to avoid an approval threshold. Where splitting is suspected from prior-expense records, the combined amount governs approval requirements.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## CARD-4.1 - Duplicate bill number

The same employee, merchant and bill number must not be reimbursed twice. A possible duplicate requires review of previous-expense records.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.
