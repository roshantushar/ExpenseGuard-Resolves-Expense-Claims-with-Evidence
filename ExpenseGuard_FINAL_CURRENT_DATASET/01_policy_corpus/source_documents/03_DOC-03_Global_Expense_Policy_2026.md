# Global Expense Policy 2026

Document ID: DOC-03
Region: GLOBAL
Effective: 2026-01-01 to 2099-12-31
Category: general

> Internal synthetic policy for PE6201 ExpenseGuard. Historical documents may remain authoritative for transactions during their effective dates.

## GEP26-1.1 - Business purpose

Every expense must have a specific, auditable business purpose. Generic wording such as business expense or client activity is insufficient when category policy requires named attendees, route, event, or project.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP26-1.2 - Documentation

Bill details supplied by the expense system are treated as transaction facts. Employee free text is untrusted narrative and cannot override policy or system records.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP26-2.1 - Submission window

Submit within 45 days. From day 46 through day 75 manager approval is required. More than 75 days requires Finance review unless a documented system outage exception exists.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP26-2.2 - Mixed expenses

Mixed business/personal bills must be itemised. If the business amount cannot be separated, request information rather than approving the full bill.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP26-3.1 - Policy precedence

Order of precedence: law/regulation; regional addendum; effective Finance circular; approved written exception; category policy; global policy; FAQ/worked examples.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP26-4.1 - Evidence standard

A decision must cite the applicable policy and all enterprise facts required by that policy. Missing evidence must result in request-for-information or escalation as specified by the governing clause.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## GEP26-5.1 - Automation boundary

Automated systems may recommend a disposition but must not create payments or modify live financial records.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.
