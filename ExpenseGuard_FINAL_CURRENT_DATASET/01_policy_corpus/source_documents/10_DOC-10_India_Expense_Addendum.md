# India Expense Addendum

Document ID: DOC-10
Region: IN
Effective: 2024-01-01 to 2099-12-31
Category: regional

> Internal synthetic policy for PE6201 ExpenseGuard. Historical documents may remain authoritative for transactions during their effective dates.

## IN-1.1 - Employee meal ceiling

India employee-only meal ceiling is INR 1800 per person in 2024, INR 2000 in 2025, and INR 2200 in 2026.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## IN-1.2 - Client meal ceiling

India client meal ceiling is INR 4500 per attendee in 2024-2025 and INR 5000 in 2026.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## IN-2.1 - Alcohol prohibition

Alcohol is not reimbursable for India transactions, including client entertainment, regardless of manager approval.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## IN-3.1 - Local transport

App-based taxi is reimbursable for approved business travel; normal commute is not.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## IN-4.1 - Regional precedence

The alcohol prohibition and local monetary ceilings override conflicting global meal rules.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.
