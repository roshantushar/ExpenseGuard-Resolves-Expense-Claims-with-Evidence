# Training and Conferences Policy

Document ID: DOC-08
Region: GLOBAL
Effective: 2024-01-01 to 2099-12-31
Category: conference

> Internal synthetic policy for PE6201 ExpenseGuard. Historical documents may remain authoritative for transactions during their effective dates.

## CONF-1.1 - Registration

Conference fees are reimbursable only for approved professional events relevant to the employee role or project.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## CONF-1.2 - Approved event evidence

Use the conference registry or approved travel record to establish event approval. Employee description alone is insufficient when an exception depends on event status.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## CONF-2.1 - Partner hotel exception

For an approved conference, an official partner hotel may exceed the normal regional hotel ceiling by up to 20 percent in 2024-2025 and 25 percent in 2026.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## CONF-2.2 - Non-partner hotel

A hotel that is not an official partner remains subject to the normal hotel ceiling unless a separate approved exception exists.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## CONF-3.1 - Training meals

Meals included in a conference fee cannot also be claimed separately. Where inclusion is unclear, request information.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## CONF-4.1 - Certification exam

Professional certification exam fees require manager approval and an active role-development plan.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.
