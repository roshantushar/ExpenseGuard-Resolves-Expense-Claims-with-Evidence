# Travel and Hotel Policy

Document ID: DOC-04
Region: GLOBAL
Effective: 2024-01-01 to 2099-12-31
Category: travel

> Internal synthetic policy for PE6201 ExpenseGuard. Historical documents may remain authoritative for transactions during their effective dates.

## TRV-1.1 - Travel authorisation

Airfare and hotel expenses require an approved business trip unless a category-specific exception explicitly removes this requirement.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## TRV-1.2 - Hotel ceilings

Hotel reimbursement is limited by transaction year, location, and employee grade. Use the Historical Limits document for the applicable base ceiling.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## TRV-1.3 - Conference lodging

Where an approved conference is identified, consult the Training and Conferences Policy before rejecting a hotel solely for exceeding the normal ceiling.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## TRV-2.1 - Airfare cabin

Economy class is standard. Premium economy is allowed for scheduled flight time above 6 hours for G4+ employees. Business class requires G7/EXEC or an approved medical/travel exception.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## TRV-3.1 - Ground transport

Taxi or ride-hailing is reimbursable for business travel between business locations, airport, hotel, client site, or approved event. Normal home-to-office commuting is not reimbursable.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## TRV-3.2 - Late-night transport

Home transport after 22:00 may be reimbursable when required by documented business activity ending after 21:30; route details are required.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## TRV-4.1 - Personal extension

Additional cost caused by a personal travel extension is not reimbursable. If fare comparison is missing, request information.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## TRV-5.1 - Travel evidence

When approval status or destination is material to a rule, use the authoritative travel-request record rather than employee narrative.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.
