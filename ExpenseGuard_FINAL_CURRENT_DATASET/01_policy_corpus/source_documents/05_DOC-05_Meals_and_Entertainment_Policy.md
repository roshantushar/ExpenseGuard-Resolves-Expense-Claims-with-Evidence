# Meals and Entertainment Policy

Document ID: DOC-05
Region: GLOBAL
Effective: 2024-01-01 to 2099-12-31
Category: meals

> Internal synthetic policy for PE6201 ExpenseGuard. Historical documents may remain authoritative for transactions during their effective dates.

## MEAL-1.1 - Employee meal

A meal consumed only by employees during ordinary work is limited to the applicable regional employee-meal ceiling and requires a business reason beyond routine attendance.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## MEAL-1.2 - Client meal

A client meal requires the names or organisations of external attendees and a business purpose. Per-attendee spend is calculated using all attendees present, including employees.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## MEAL-1.3 - Business entertainment

External entertainment that is primarily recreational uses the entertainment ceiling, not the client-meal ceiling, and requires manager approval above the standard threshold.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## MEAL-2.1 - Alcohol

Alcohol follows the regional addendum. Where permitted, it must be incidental to an otherwise reimbursable meal or event.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## MEAL-2.2 - Tips and service charges

Reasonable mandatory service charges are reimbursable. Voluntary tips above 20 percent require manager approval unless a regional rule is stricter.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## MEAL-3.1 - Attendee evidence

If attendee count is required to calculate a per-person threshold and cannot be established, request information.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## MEAL-4.1 - Mixed attendee purpose

Where an event combines employee-only and external-client activity and the allocation is unclear, request clarification rather than selecting the more generous limit.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.
