# Japan Expense Addendum

Document ID: DOC-11
Region: JP
Effective: 2024-01-01 to 2099-12-31
Category: regional

> Internal synthetic policy for PE6201 ExpenseGuard. Historical documents may remain authoritative for transactions during their effective dates.

## JP-1.1 - Employee meal ceiling

Japan employee-only meal ceiling is JPY 4500 in 2024, JPY 5000 in 2025, and JPY 5500 in 2026.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## JP-1.2 - Client meal ceiling

Japan client meal ceiling is JPY 12000 per attendee in 2024-2025 and JPY 13000 in 2026.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## JP-2.1 - Tokyo hotel note

Tokyo uses the metropolitan hotel ceiling in the Historical Limits document. Other Japanese cities use the Japan-other ceiling.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## JP-2.2 - Country-manager approval

Client entertainment above JPY 60000 total requires country-manager approval even when per-attendee spend is within the normal ceiling.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## JP-3.1 - Rail

Shinkansen ordinary reserved seating is reimbursable for approved business travel. Green Car requires G6+ or documented exception.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.

## JP-4.1 - Regional precedence

Japan monetary thresholds override global generic thresholds for Japan transactions.

Operational note: apply this clause only when its scope, effective date, regional precedence and required evidence are satisfied.
