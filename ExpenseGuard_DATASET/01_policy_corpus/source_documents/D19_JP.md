## JP-1.1 - Scope and precedence

This addendum applies to transactions incurred in Japan. Where it differs from a global policy for the same subject, this addendum controls, and a Finance circular that names one of its clauses amends that clause from the effective date.

Interpretive guidance: The purpose of this addendum is to clarify the applicability of specific policies to transactions incurred in Japan, establishing precedence over global policies when discrepancies arise. This is particularly relevant in business situations where employees are operating in Japan and may encounter unique regulatory or cultural considerations that necessitate tailored guidelines. By delineating the scope of this addendum, the company aims to provide clear direction for employees and ensure compliance with local practices.

A common misunderstanding is that employees may not fully recognize the implications of this addendum, assuming that global policies apply uniformly without regard for local variations. It is important to understand that this addendum serves to align the company's policies with local laws and customs, thereby enhancing operational effectiveness. This topic is conceptually linked to other regional policies, as both aim to ensure that employees are equipped with the necessary guidance to navigate the complexities of conducting business in diverse environments.

## JP-2.1 - Employee meal ceiling

| Year | Ceiling (JPY) |
|---|---|
| 2024 | 4,500 |
| 2025 | 5,000 |
| 2026 | 5,500 |

Interpretive guidance: The clause regarding the employee meal ceiling establishes a financial limit for meals consumed by employees during business-related activities. A common misunderstanding surrounding the employee meal ceiling is the belief that it applies uniformly to all meal types or settings, when in fact it specifically pertains to meals consumed by employees in a business context. This clause is conceptually linked to other expense policies, such as those governing client meals and entertainment expenses, which have distinct ceilings and purposes. Employees should be aware that exceeding the established ceiling may result in non-reimbursement, emphasizing the importance of adhering to the specified limits.
