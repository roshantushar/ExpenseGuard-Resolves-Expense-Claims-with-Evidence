# Definitions, Frequently Asked Questions and Worked Examples

Document ID: D22
Region: GLOBAL
Effective: 2024-01-01 to 2099-12-31
Category: faq

> Internal policy document of Northstar Analytics Group. Precedence rank 7 (lower rank overrides higher rank where documents differ).

## DEF-1.1 - Calendar day

Deadlines are counted in calendar days including weekends and public holidays, from the transaction date to the submission date.

## DEF-1.2 - External attendee

A person who is not an employee of the company or its subsidiaries.

## DEF-1.3 - Approver level

The seniority of an approver: MANAGER, DIRECTOR or FINANCE, in ascending order.

## DEF-1.4 - Cost centre and project

A cost centre is a budget holder. A project is a work order charged to a cost centre, optionally under a parent project.

## DEF-1.5 - Grade band

Grades are grouped into bands used in ceiling tables.

## DEF-1.6 - Nightly rate

The total accommodation charge divided by the number of nights.

## FAQ-1.1 - Question 1

Q: Who decides when two policies appear to conflict?

A: The order of precedence in the global policy decides. Where precedence cannot be determined from published text the claim goes to Finance.

## FAQ-1.2 - Question 2

Q: Does an employee explanation ever replace a system record?

A: No. Where an authoritative record exists it controls. The explanation is used to understand the purpose of the expense.

## FAQ-1.3 - Question 3

Q: What is a discretionary expense?

A: An expense the employee chose to incur within business purpose, as opposed to a mandatory statutory charge or a cost fixed by contract.

## FAQ-1.4 - Question 4

Q: Are taxes included in the amount tested against a ceiling?

A: The total on the bill, including taxes and mandatory service charges, is tested, unless the category policy says the nightly rate or the pre-tip bill is used.

## FAQ-1.5 - Question 5

Q: Can a claim be partly approved?

A: A claim receives one disposition. Where the business portion cannot be separated, or the claim exceeds a computed amount, the claim is returned or rejected as the category policy states.

## FAQ-1.6 - Question 6

Q: What happens when a cited exception cannot be found?

A: The reviewer requests the correct reference from the employee.

## FAQ-1.7 - Question 7

Q: Is a manager approval given after the expense valid?

A: Approval validity is tested against the transaction date and the approval status, as the approval policy sets out.

## FAQ-1.8 - Question 8

Q: Can a manager delegate approval while travelling?

A: Yes, through a recorded delegation with a validity period and a limit, as the approval policy describes.

## FAQ-1.9 - Question 9

Q: How are recurring charges treated?

A: A repeat that follows the normal interval and the same purpose is not a duplicate; the controls policy sets the interval.

## FAQ-1.10 - Question 10

Q: Do regional addenda apply to an employee based in one country who spends in another?

A: The addendum of the country where the expense was incurred applies.

## FAQ-1.11 - Question 11

Q: Are contractors and freelancers external attendees at a meal?

A: Yes. Only people on the payroll of the company or a subsidiary count as employees; contractors, freelancers and agency staff are external.

## FAQ-1.12 - Question 12

Q: Is a prepaid e-voucher or a store credit a cash equivalent?

A: Yes. Any stored-value or exchangeable instrument is a cash equivalent, whatever the medium.

## FAQ-1.13 - Question 13

Q: Is a public university or a state-owned company a government-affiliated body for gift purposes?

A: Yes. Public bodies and companies owned or controlled by the state are government-affiliated even when they operate commercially.

## FAQ-1.14 - Question 14

Q: Which document is authoritative for grade?

A: The employee profile record, not the claim.

## FAQ-1.15 - Question 15

Q: Are tips reimbursable in every country?

A: The meals policy and the regional addendum decide. In some countries gratuities are not customary.

## FAQ-1.16 - Question 16

Q: What is the difference between a gift and hospitality?

A: A gift is given to keep; hospitality is a shared event such as a meal.

## FAQ-1.17 - Question 17

Q: Can a project charge to another project's budget?

A: A child project charges the cost centre of its parent project, as the budget policy describes.

## FAQ-1.18 - Question 18

Q: Where do I find a superseded value?

A: The historical schedules list the base values by year; circulars supersede them from their effective dates.
